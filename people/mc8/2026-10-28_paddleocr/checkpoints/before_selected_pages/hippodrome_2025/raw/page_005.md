## Our Business 

The Hippodrome sits on the corner of Leicester Square, in the heart of London's world-renowned West End 

The building, one of the city's most famous theatrical icons, has hosted many of the last century's groundbreaking diversions, shows and entertainments. Since opening in 19oo it has pushed boundaries, presented the bold, the new, the never-seen-before.
