## Notes on reconciling items:

EDF Energy Customers Limited is also engaged in wider business activities beyond licensed energy supply. To ensure the Css only captures the licensed energy supply business, these wider activities are not included in the figures reported within the Css under the licensed energy supply activities (e.g. gas and electricity supply). Impairment charges and restructuring costs are also not included in the figures reported under gas and electricity supply in accordance with Ofgem guidelines.
