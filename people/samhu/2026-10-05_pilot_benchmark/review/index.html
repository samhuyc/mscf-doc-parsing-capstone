How to read these reviews

  • Counts are passed checks / applicable checks, not a percentage of the entire document.
  • The same number may pass retention and fail table association; these answer different questions.
  • Missing or ambiguous matches fail applicable checks. N/A means not measured, not zero performance.
  • There is no combined score. Small, selected samples do not establish overall parser quality.
