Metric glossary & label JSON examples

A label records the expected source content; a metric tests one aspect of the extracted output. One label can generate several checks. Every entry below includes its definition, how its fields are used, and a complete real JSON record from labels.jsonl.

Most scores are passed / applicable checks; higher is better. Character error rate is lower-is-better. Precision and recall count numeric occurrences. Missing or ambiguous matches fail applicable checks; N/A means not measured. These selected draft references are not independently verified gold.
