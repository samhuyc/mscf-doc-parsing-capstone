Document mean

C = Σ wᵢpᵢ / Σ wᵢ

Weights count output characters. Longer text contributes more. This measures average certainty over emitted text.

Document geometric mean

G = exp(mean(log pᵢ))

Each unit has equal weight. Low probabilities pull the score down more strongly. A zero score makes G zero.

These are candidate quality statistics. Neither formula establishes P(document correct), accounts for omitted text, or makes confidence scales comparable across models. MinerU here performs full-page recognition, excluding its complete layout/table pipeline.

04Does higher confidence rank better output?

A diagnostic comparison of the completed rerun pages.

The deadline run prioritizes existing reference pages and a known failure. This selected sample cannot establish generalization. Confidence never uses reference text.

Spearman measures ranking, not probability calibration. +1 means rankings agree, −1 means they reverse. Results are descriptive, with no held-out calibration claim.

Full-page agreement = max(0, 1 − CER), where CER is character edit distance divided by reference length after case/whitespace normalization. CER can exceed 100% when output contains extensive repetition. Reading-order and Markdown differences also count as edits.
