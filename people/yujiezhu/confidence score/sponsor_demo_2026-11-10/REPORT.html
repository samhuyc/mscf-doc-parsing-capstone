Two different questions

Is the numeric content correct? Does the exported cell also preserve the correct row, column and unit context? Here, 45/50 cells preserve numeric content and 30/50 pass the stricter structured-cell contract. These are sample counts, not probabilities assigned to individual cells.

Observable evidence

Stronger evidence requires one well-formed value, unmerged value/row cells, a unique comparison row and agreement on value and a bound column header. Otherwise evidence is weaker. Unit correctness is reviewed but has no independent predictive feature in this version. Agreement can occur when both parsers are wrong.

Separate-document replay

30 development cells and 20 retrospective replay cells. Both related Rolls-Royce documents remain together. Earlier reports were known; this is not a blind test.

  • Stronger evidence: 10/10 replay cells pass the strict contract.
  • Weaker evidence: 0/10 replay cells pass the strict contract.

All ten sampled release numbers are right, but the primary export merges their period headers. A strict structural failure is not proof of a numerically wrong value. Table-level errors make the cell observations correlated.

Separate correctness models are now implemented

Model results and validation audit · Human-review queue

The new pipeline fits individual-feature logistic models for numeric transcription and row/column association, with document-family validation and separate calibration support. Numeric validation covers 40/50 cells but zero of the five errors: holding EDF out removes all failures from training. Association validation covers all 50 cells but struggles on the Rolls-Royce merged headers. These are exploratory diagnostics; public probabilities remain unavailable.

What would make a probability meaningful?

Define numeric correctness separately from association correctness. Collect independent labels across representative documents, retain individual evidence features, fit a correctness model, and evaluate/calibrate it on document-separated data. On genuinely new documents, items assigned probabilities near 0.95 should be correct approximately 95% of the time. More distinct scores alone do not establish this property.

When the system cannot form a cell

Peterborough page 2 collapses to one column: review required, confidence null. It is outside the 50-cell denominator. Missing values and whole-document completeness are not measured.
